CHRONOLOGY

My Journey & Milestones

From Lead Test engineer at Tech Mahindra to founding Infipre IT Services in Sanquelim, Goa. A timeline of career evolution, academic excellence, and venture building.

2024

MD at Infipre IT Services & Founder of Mangalkaarya

Appointed Managing Director at Infipre IT Services Pvt Ltd. Founded Mangalkaarya / Illuminaires, expanding into social impact and matrimonial platforms.

2022

Founder at MAYA HR Solutions

Established MAYA HR Solutions to bridge talent acquisition gaps and deliver strategic human resource management across Goa and regional markets.

2021

Industry Expert — Internal Quality Audit Committee

Appointed Industry Expert on the Internal Quality Audit Committee at Government College of Sanquelim, advising on academic-industry alignment.

2020

Ventured into Cyber Security & MBA Strategic Mgmt

Expanded operations into enterprise cyber security audit & compliance. Inducted into GopalKrishna Vidyaprasarak Sanstha. Earned MBA in Strategic Management from MIT Pune.

2019

Launch of SCRRAPY ERP

Architected and launched SCRRAPY, a revolutionary enterprise ERP platform designed for inventory management, manufacturing, and trading business automation.

2017

CEO & Founder (Infipre IT Services) & ISTQB Certified

Founded Infipre IT Services OPC Pvt Ltd in Sanquelim, Goa. Certified at Foundation Level by ISTQB (International Software Testing Qualifications Board). Completed MBA in Finance (MIT Pune).

2014

MBA in Information Technology (IT)

Graduated with MBA in Information Technology from Maharashtra Institute of Technology (MIT Pune), mastering software architecture and technology management.

2010

Lead Test at Tech Mahindra

Served as Test Lead at Tech Mahindra, leading Quality Assurance and testing operations on the landmark Reliance Jio Launch project.
