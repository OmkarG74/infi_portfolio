BACKGROUND & FOUNDING STORY

Returning to Roots to Build Sustainable Technology

Yatin Sanjeev Kanekar is a software executive, product architect, and entrepreneur with over 14 years of hands-on experience in Quality Assurance, enterprise software engineering, and digital transformation.

Beginning his professional career as a Lead Test engineer at Tech Mahindra—where he spearheaded QA operations for the landmark Reliance Jio Launch project—Yatin learned the intricacies of building high-scale, mission-critical systems. However, driven by a deep conviction to contribute to his hometown and revive his family’s entrepreneurial legacy, he returned to Sanquelim, Goa in 2016.

In 2017, he founded Infipre IT Services OPC Pvt Ltd, proving that institutional-grade custom software, ERP solutions, and testing services can be built from non-metro regions without compromising on quality or ethics.
